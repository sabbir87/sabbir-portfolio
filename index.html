Personal Details & Background

Core personal details, family background, campus roles, and a quick look at what keeps me busy.

I am Sabbir Ahommed, originally from Shahagola, Atrai, Naogaon, and currently based around Daffodil Smart City in Birulia, Savar, Dhaka. At Daffodil International University, I study Computer Science and Engineering in the 64th Batch and work as a Student Associate.

In scouting, I serve as Senior Rover Mate and Vice President of the Crew Council at the DIU Air Rover Scout Group. My involvement includes camp organization, 150 km rambling expeditions, pioneering, Safe from Harm and JOTA-JOTI activities, campus volunteer coordination, and representing Bangladesh Scouts as a volunteer delegate at the BIMSTEC Conference 2025 in New Delhi.

On the technical and creative side, I build web applications, database-backed lab systems, 3D OpenGL simulations, and machine learning models for phishing detection and healthcare risk prediction, alongside designing custom sublimation jerseys, tracksuits, and event graphics.
